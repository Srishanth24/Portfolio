Engineering Perspective

About My Engineering Focus

I approach software engineering from a systems and delivery mindset. Rather than viewing frontend, backend, and artificial intelligence as isolated domains, I enjoy building end-to-end architectures where data models are clean, APIs are predictable, cloud deployments are reliable, and AI workflows are constrained by validation gates.

My primary interest is in Backend Software Engineering—specifically building reliable, maintainable services using Java and Spring Boot, structured SQL databases, and cloud components. In parallel, I am interested in Forward Deployed Engineering, where technical craftsmanship pairs directly with dissecting real customer requirements, iterating rapidly, and delivering software that solves practical problems.

When working with Generative AI, my focus is on structured workflows—using LangGraph to coordinate state transitions, RAG pipelines with vector databases to ground outputs in real documents, and review agents to check consistency before outputs reach users.
